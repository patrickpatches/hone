The colour call

Fewer colours, each with a job

One accent, neutral everything else

The clash came from three accents at once (gold + green + rust) and from colouring the icons. A world-class palette is restrained: structure stays quiet, and colour is saved for the one thing you act on.

Dark sage — surfaces. The room.
Warm cream — text. Names you read.
Soft cream — icons. Quiet wayfinding, not status.
Gold — the one accent, on the stepper you touch.

Why green came off: in a "what you have" list every row is something you own — so a green icon on every row signals nothing, it's just noise. Neutral icons let the gold stepper be the only thing that pops. Green stays reserved for true "done" moments (cook mode), where it actually means something. No token changes to the locked theme — this just stops painting icons with accent colours.
