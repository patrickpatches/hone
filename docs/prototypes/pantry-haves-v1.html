The colour call

Each colour, one job

Calm by default, colour with meaning

The earlier clash came from three accents at once and from colouring the icons. Now structure is quiet and colour is rationed to where it carries information.

Dark sage — surfaces.
Warm cream — text you read.
Soft cream — icons. Quiet wayfinding, not status.
Gold — the one thing you tap (the stepper).
Green — one job only: a recipe that's ready to cook.
Why green is back — but only here. On the ingredient rows, green was noise (every row is something you own). On a recipe card, "Ready to cook" is a genuine go-signal — that's the one place a green pill earns its meaning. Icons stay neutral; "still need" chips stay neutral; nothing competes. No locked-token changes — green is used once, intentionally, not sprinkled.
