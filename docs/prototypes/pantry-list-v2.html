The piece that was missing

"What can I cook?" — back in

The match carousel, kept

Same behaviour as the current build: every recipe is scored against your pantry and ranked by how much you already have. Cards you can make fully show a sage "Tap to cook"; the close ones show "X of Y matched" and a row of "still need" chips that drop the missing items onto your shopping list.

This is the existing engine. scoreRecipeAgainstPantry already ranks recipes by coverage and even matches derived ingredients (it knows "egg yolks" are covered if you have eggs, tagged "from your eggs"). I only moved it to the top and restyled the cards in the v2 tokens — no new matching logic. Placement: matches go ABOVE the ingredient list because the pantry's whole promise is "cook with what you have" — the answer should be the first thing you see, not the last. The list below is the management area.

Buy what the shop sells

Real units, not recipe grams

Tomatoes are 2, not 200 g

Recipes are written in cooking units; shoppers think in shopping units. Each ingredient carries the unit you actually buy it in.

Honesty kept (golden rule 5): weight→count is an estimate, marked "≈". Bulk-by-weight items stay in grams. We never invent a precise number we can't stand behind. New work: a small per-ingredient "buy unit" reference (count / weight / pack) + average weights for the count conversions. ~50–60 common ingredients; the rest fall back to the recipe unit.
