Combine off the heat
Pull the pan off the heat. Wait 30 seconds — let it cool just enough. Add the hot pasta and toss hard. Pour in the egg paste gradually, tossing continuously. The steam from the pasta does the cooking.
Look for this
The sauce should coat every strand and look glossy and pale golden. If it looks scrambled or grainy, the pan was too hot. If it looks wet and loose, keep tossing.
Why this matters
Eggs scramble at 70°C. Off-heat is the only safe way to emulsify — the residual warmth is your cooking medium.

What this changes and why

The giant step number

108sp Playfair Display in near-black — it's a typographic element, not a UI label. Borrowed from editorial magazine chapter numbers. The "of 5" label in ink3 next to it gives context without visual weight. Your eye goes: big number → title → body. That's exactly the read order you want when your hands smell like guanciale.

Doneness cue in gold

Gold left-border card, italic text. This is the single most important sentence on the screen — more important than the instruction. The timer tells you when to check; this tells you what to look for when you do. Every other recipe app buries this in the step body. Pulling it out and colouring it gold means a user who's half-watching the pan can find the cue in one glance.

The "why" note in italic

Playfair italic, ink3. Visually it reads like a chef's aside — quieter than the instruction, but present if you want it. This is the pedagogical layer of the app: not just what to do, but why. The italic treatment is borrowed directly from cookbook sidebars. It answers the question without interrupting the flow.

True black OLED background

Cook mode uses #000000 not the dark sage background. The spec already calls for this (CLAUDE.md: "Dark mode default in cook mode. OLED-friendly true blacks"). In a kitchen at night, a black screen is invisible as ambient light. The surface cards sit on black rather than on the sage-tinted dark, making the content areas pop with more contrast.

Ingredient callout chips

Small pills listing the ingredients used in this step. Removes the need to scroll back to the ingredients list mid-cook. Clicking one could show the substitution sheet — an interaction the engineer can wire after the visual ships.

Progress segments, not a bar

Five individual segments fill left-to-right in gold as you progress. Gives a stronger sense of position than a continuous bar — you know exactly how many steps remain. The label "Step 3 of 5" below reinforces it in text for accessibility. The final step shows all five segments filled before you tap "Finished."
