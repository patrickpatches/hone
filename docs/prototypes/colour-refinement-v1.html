Colour Refinement v1 · refine, don't redirect

Two targets Patrick named, side by side

An upgrade of the current dark-sage look — not a fifth direction. Only colour and emphasis change. No new fonts, no new layout, no new direction. Sage stays the app's "done / checked" colour; gold stays the brand accent; rust stays the primary action.

What Patrick said: "some of the green font and pills aren't the best choice… and the 'cook with what you have' / 'cook with what you buy' text doesn't pop." Both are addressed below, each as a before/after on the real screen.

Target 2 · pantry-mode entry strings

"Cook with what you have / buy" — make them earn their place

These two lines are the gateway to the pantry-first kill feature, but today they're set as a tiny sage eyebrow — and that sage fails contrast (3.7:1, below the 4.5:1 readability floor), which is exactly why they read flat. Two treatments below; the recommended one is on the right.

What shifts: the entry strings move from low-contrast sage to gold — either two-tone text (A, recommended) or a contained gold pill (B). What stays: the words, the position above the title, and the Playfair title itself. No layout change: same text, same place, recoloured. · Engineer cost: two string styles in pantry.tsx + shop.tsx (and the title's italic colour). ~15 minutes.
Optional refinement · same flavour

Step-number badge — fix the light-on-light number

A known issue, already queued for the engineer: in the recipe Method list the step number is near-invisible (cream number on a cream circle). The colour intent is simply a dark number. This documents the shade so it lands right.

Before
1
Brown the mince. Get the pan screaming hot, break the mince in, and leave it to catch colour before you stir.

The "1" is there — cream on cream. Contrast ~1.1:1. Effectively invisible.

After
1
Brown the mince. Get the pan screaming hot, break the mince in, and leave it to catch colour before you stir.

Dark charcoal ink (#141414) on the cream circle. Contrast 13.8:1 — well past AA. The badge keeps its warm cream fill; only the number darkens.

What shifts: the step number's colour, from cream to charcoal ink. What stays: the cream circle, the size, the position. This is browse-mode only — cook mode's ghost watermark number is intentional and untouched. Engineer cost: one colour on the badge text in recipe/[id].tsx (Method list). ~5 minutes. Already on the engineer's open list — this just pins the exact shade.
